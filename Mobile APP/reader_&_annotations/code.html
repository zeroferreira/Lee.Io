As the reader continues down the page, the high-contrast theme ensures that eye strain is minimized, especially in low-light environments. Every detail, from the serif font choice to the line spacing, is crafted for long-form immersion.

Transitioning between chapters should feel like turning a physical page. The digital medium offers us the chance to enhance the reading experience without cluttering the canvas. We use primary blue accents only where attention is required—syncing status, progress milestones, and active tools.

In the end, a reader is a sanctuary. A tool for the mind that respects the silence required for deep thought. The interface must be a servant to the content, stepping back whenever the user is engaged.
